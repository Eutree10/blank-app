Fuel, recovery & red flags

Nutrition (15 y/o endurance athlete)

  • Don't chase weight — 65 kg is a healthy racing weight. Under-fuelling (RED-S) stalls growth and performance.
  • Carbs are your main fuel — rice, pasta, oats, potatoes, bread, fruit. Refuel within ~60 min of key sessions.
  • Protein ~1.4–1.6 g/kg/day (~90–105 g) across the day.
  • Iron — teen runners are highest-risk for low ferritin. Iron-rich foods + vitamin C; consider a baseline ferritin blood test.
  • Calcium + vitamin D for growing, loading bones.

Recovery & monitoring

  • Sleep 8.5 h is excellent — protect it. It's where adaptation happens at 15.
  • Track morning resting HR (baseline 53). A sustained rise of ~7+ bpm = back off.
  • Keep easy days genuinely easy (≤160 bpm). The talk test is your guide.

Red flags

  • No raw Garmin export was parsed — built from your race PBs. Re-test paces as you improve.
  • The humidity+rain 10k is unusable — for a real endurance read, race a 10k or hard tempo in cool conditions.
  • A baseline ferritin test is cheap insurance for a teen distance runner.
  • You're 15 and growing — hold volume at 45–55 km, take down weeks, do strength work. Durability first.
Coaching framework, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before ramping training or blood testing (you're a minor).
